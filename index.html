01 / The idea

Turn creative attacks
into useful evidence.

How much effort does it take to elicit misaligned capabilities from open-weight models? Join people asking the same question, try an approach, and help build a clearer picture of misuse risk.

01

Explore the frontier.

Investigate the relationship between what an attack can achieve and the effort it takes. Bring an idea you want to put to the test.

02

Build with others.

Work with a team, exchange research practices, and learn alongside people interested in the safety of open-weight AI.

03

Make it reproducible.

Document what you tried and what you found. Contribute evidence that helps others understand, reproduce, and build on the findings.

People with an interest in AI safety, red-teaming, and hands-on research. Programming or machine learning experience will be useful. We welcome interest from both experienced practitioners and people earlier in their research careers.

You can express interest on your own or with teammates. We’ll share more about team formation ahead of the event.

We’ll share event logistics ahead of November 7 and introduce the technical task at the hackathon.
